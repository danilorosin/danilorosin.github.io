Welcome!

I’m a PhD Candidate in Economics under the supervision of Professor Massimiliano Tancioni (Sapienza University of Rome) and Professor Alessio Terzi (Cambridge University).

My research interest concerns the economic impacts of climate change, with particular emphasis on climate shocks and climate policies. This topic first emerged in my master’s thesis, Temporary and Permanent Temperature Shocks and the Transmission on Energy Prices: a Bayesian Panel VAR Approach, and has since evolved into the three chapters of my doctoral dissertation, Climate Shocks and Climate Policy: Essays in Environmental Economics.

I believe economics offers powerful tools to help address the urgent challenges that climate change poses, and I’m committed in giving my small contribution toward building a more sustainable future.

Currently, I'm a Ph.D. Researcher at the Research Department of the Italian Confederation of Industry (Centro Studi Confindustria).
